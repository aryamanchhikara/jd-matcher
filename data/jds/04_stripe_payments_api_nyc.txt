We take API design seriously: backwards compatibility is a hard constraint, latency budgets are tight, and correctness matters more than shipping fast.

- Own the operational health of the services you write, including on-call.
- Write documentation and code samples that engineers on the other side of the API will actually read.
- Participate in Stripe's rigorous API review process.

We're looking for
- 3+ years of professional backend engineering experience.
- Proficiency in at least one of Ruby, Go, Java, or a similar server-side language, with the ability to pick up the others.
- Experience designing external-facing APIs (REST or gRPC) that other engineers integrate against.
- A high bar for correctness in the presence of concurrency and partial failure.
