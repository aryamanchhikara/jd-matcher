Figma — Product Engineer, Design Systems
Location: New York, NY or San Francisco, CA (hybrid, 3 days/week)
Level: Mid (3+ years)

About the role
The Design Systems team builds the tools designers use to define, govern, and roll out design languages across their organizations. This is one of the highest-leverage surfaces in the product: a small change here can ripple through thousands of teams.

You will
- Ship user-facing features in TypeScript and React across the Figma editor and web app.
- Occasionally work in the C++ rendering core to add editor support for new primitives.
- Partner with designers and PMs from problem framing through launch.
- Instrument and analyze feature usage to inform iteration.

Requirements
- 3+ years shipping consumer or prosumer web software.
- Strong TypeScript. Working proficiency in React.
- Willingness to read and modify C++ occasionally. You don't need to be a systems engineer, but "I'll never touch it" is not the right attitude.
- Design taste, or at least the humility to defer to designers who have it.

Work arrangement
Hybrid: 3 days per week in the New York or San Francisco office.
