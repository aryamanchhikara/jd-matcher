About the team
The Metrics Ingest team owns the highest-throughput write path at Datadog: tens of millions of metric points per second, ingested, aggregated, and durably stored under strict tail-latency SLOs. We run our own storage engine on bare metal and hybrid cloud. If a customer's dashboard is slow, or a data point is missing, our team owns it.

What you'll do
- Design and implement backend services in Go that sit on the hottest write path in the product.
- Own components end-to-end: capacity planning, on-call rotation, incident review, and performance tuning at the kernel-and-network layer.
- Improve throughput and reduce per-point CPU cost through profiling, allocation reduction, and careful concurrency design.
- Collaborate with the storage, query, and reliability teams on cross-cutting problems.
- Mentor mid-level and junior engineers; drive design reviews.

- Solid grounding in distributed systems fundamentals: consistency, partitioning, backpressure, quorum, at-least-once vs exactly-once semantics.
- Comfort with Linux performance tools (perf, eBPF, flame graphs) and a debugging approach that goes below the language runtime.
- Experience being primary on-call for a production system.
